Kaluga,

Dear Sir:

We have gone over your pamphlets on
metal airship construction which have been referred
to us by the Ford Motor Company, and appreciate the
opportunity of examining your design.

We are, however, actively engaged in
the construction of a metalclad airship of our own
design for the U.S. Navy, and are not in a position
where we could use your ideas in any way.

Enclosed herewith are the pamphlets,

Yours very truly,

AIRCRAFT DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION
R. H. Upson[?]
