Enclosure.

February 27th 1934.

Dear Sir,

It is an established rule that no scientific communication
or paper is considered, read or published by the Royal Society
unless it has been communicated by a Fellow or Foreign Member
of the Society, whose duty it shall be "to satisfy himself that
any letter, report or other paper which he may communicate is
suitable to be read before the Society".

I am therefore returning your manuscript herewith.

Yours faithfully,

R. WINCKWORTH [подпись: RW]
